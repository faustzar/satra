Active Identification Standard:
South African Standard (1-2-4-7 Additive)
Official SA Protocol

Standard South African decimal additive scheme. Marginals hold place values (Units 1-2-4-7, Tens 10-20-40-70, Hundreds 100-200-400-700, Thousands 1000-2000-4000-7000). The sum of notched marginals gives the specimen's unique identification number.

Decimal Additive Principle:
Any digit from 1 to 9 is formed by at most 2 notches:
• 1=1, 2=2, 3=1+2, 4=4, 5=1+4, 6=2+4, 7=7, 8=1+7, 9=2+7
• Example from standard: Specimen 56 = (10+40) on left anterior + (2+4) on right anterior.
Official Cape Nature / SANBI Guidelines: Bridge marginals (bridge scutes) are preserved to prevent structural shell weakness. Triangular notches are filed into outer keratin.
